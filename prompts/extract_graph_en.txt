
-Goal-
Given an aviation accident report, identify ONLY the **most critical** entities and causal relationships. Focus on the "Human-Machine-Environment-Management" (H-M-E-M) framework. Construct a clear causal chain [Factor] -> [Event] -> [Outcome].

-Entity Types-
- person: Specific named individuals only. (e.g., Zhang Wei).
- equipment: Specific aircraft parts or systems. (e.g., Left wing fuel line, No.1 Engine).
- environment: Specific weather or physical conditions. (e.g., Microburst, Migratory birds).
- organization: Specific airlines or agencies. (e.g., Southern Airlines).
- procedure: Specific SOPs or manuals mentioned. (e.g., Fuel check procedure, Steep turn go-around procedure).
- event: Major discrete occurrences. (e.g., Fuel leak, Tail strike, Engine fire, Forced landing).
- violation: Specific regulatory breaches.

-Relationship Types-
- leads to / causes: [Cause] -> [Effect].
- limited by: [Entity] -> [Constraint].
- violates: [Person/Action] -> [Procedure].
- belongs to: [Member] -> [Organization].

-Steps-
1. Identify **core** entities.
- **Atomic Entities:** Split long phrases. "Left wing fuel line rupture" should be Entity "Left wing fuel line" and Relationship "leads to rupture".
- **Strict Naming:** Use specific names. **ABSOLUTELY NO GENERIC TITLES** (e.g., use "Zhang Wei", NOT "Captain"; use "Wang Fang", NOT "First Officer").
- **No Demonstratives:** Never use "The component", "This system", etc.
Format: ("entity"<|><entity_name><|><entity_type><|><entity_description>)

2. Identify causal relationships.
- **Chain of Causality:** Don't skip steps. If A causes B and B causes C, extract A->B and B->C.
Format: ("relationship"<|><source_entity><|><target_entity><|><relationship_description><|><relationship_strength>)

3. **LANGUAGE & FORMAT:** 
- **Language:** All names and descriptions MUST be in English (source language).
- **Types:** Type labels MUST be in English lowercase (e.g., person, event).
- **Delimiter:** Use **##** between entries.

4. Output <|COMPLETE|>
